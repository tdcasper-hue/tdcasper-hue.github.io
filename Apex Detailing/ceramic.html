Ceramic Coatings

Enhance gloss and add durable protection to your vehicle's exterior surfaces.

Long-Term Paint Protection

Ceramic coatings create a durable protective layer over a vehicle's painted surfaces. A properly prepared and coated vehicle can be easier to maintain while displaying increased gloss and strong water-repelling characteristics.

Preparation is an important part of the process. The vehicle is thoroughly cleaned before the coating is installed so the protection bonds to a properly prepared surface.

Enhanced Gloss

Ceramic protection can enhance the depth and reflective appearance of properly prepared paint.

Easier Maintenance

Hydrophobic properties help water and contamination release more easily during routine washing.

Added Protection

A coating provides an additional sacrificial layer between the vehicle's finish and environmental contamination.

The Coating Process

  1. The vehicle receives a thorough exterior wash.
  2. Paint is inspected and prepared for protection.
  3. Additional paint enhancement may be completed when appropriate.
  4. The coating is carefully applied to the prepared surfaces.
  5. The vehicle is inspected and allowed appropriate curing time.

Coating options and preparation requirements vary depending on vehicle condition and the desired level of protection.
